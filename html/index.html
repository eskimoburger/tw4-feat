What each release added

v4.0 21 Jan 2025

  • New Oxide engine and @import "tailwindcss" (setup)
  • CSS-first config with @theme (theme)
  • @utility, @custom-variant, @reference (directives)
  • Container queries in core (container queries)
  • not-*, in-*, nth-*, **: (variants)
  • starting: and transition-discrete (motion)
  • 3D transforms (3D), conic and radial gradients (gradients)
  • inset-shadow-*, inset-ring-* (effects), field-sizing (text)

v4.1 1 Apr 2025

  • text-shadow-*, mask-*, coloured drop-shadow (effects)
  • @source not and @source inline() (setup)
  • user-valid, details-content, pointer-*, noscript (variants)
  • wrap-break-word, items-baseline-last, safe alignment (text)

v4.2 18 Feb 2026

  • Mauve, olive, mist and taupe palettes (theme)
  • First-party webpack plugin (setup)
  • Logical properties: pbs-*, inline-*, inset-s-* (text)
  • font-features-* (text)

v4.3 8 May 2026

  • scrollbar-*, zoom-*, tab-* (4.3 utilities)
  • @container-size (container queries)
  • Stacked and compound @variant, --default() (directives)
  • Patches: CLI --silent (4.3.1), bare auto-rows-12 (4.3.2), --watch --poll (4.3.3)
